Recipes

Ready-made requests to paste into your AI tool: schedule messages, watch for words, sum up groups, read documents and more. They are the same recipes the app comes with.

Once, at the set time

Schedule a message

The gateway has no scheduler, and it doesn't need one. The assistant is the one who waits: it keeps the task and, at the right time, calls send_text_message like any other call.

See the recipe Daily

Watch for keywords

A daily routine reads the whole day of conversations, looks for the terms that matter and tells you only when something turns up. The terms are yours: your company's name, a product's, your competitors', a big client's, or the words that tend to come before a problem. None of this touches the gateway: search_messages already answers the question, the rest is instructions.

See the recipe Twice a day

Summary of what's still unanswered

Lists the chats where the last message is from the other person and is a few hours old, with how many messages are waiting and since when. It's the list of who's waiting on you, and whatever you've dealt with drops off it.

See the recipe Send now, tally later

Poll and tally

Sending the poll and coming back later to read the result are two separate calls, linked by the id the first one returns.

See the recipe End of day, or on demand

A group's daily summary

A busy group piles up hundreds of messages nobody is going to read top to bottom. Taking the whole day and handing back what matters is one read call plus a request to summarize.

See the recipe On demand

Read the document or photo that came in

A PDF, a spreadsheet or a photo of a piece of paper arrives on WhatsApp, and the assistant picks up the file as it came, without you downloading and attaching it, and reads it with its own tools.
