Watch for keywords

A daily routine reads the whole day of conversations, looks for the terms that matter and tells you only when something turns up. The terms are yours: your company's name, a product's, your competitors', a big client's, or the words that tend to come before a problem. None of this touches the gateway: search_messages already answers the question, the rest is instructions.

When
Daily
Tools it uses
Search messages List chats Read a chat

The text to paste

Every day at 7 p.m., search the messages from the last 24 hours for:

business: "contract", "proposal", "invoice", "meeting", "urgent"
my company: "[company name]", "[product name]"
competitors: "[competitor 1]", "[competitor 2]"

For each match, tell me who said it, in which conversation, and the excerpt.
Group them by category. If there are none, just reply
"nothing today". Don't make up a summary.

Warning: search_messages only covers what was indexed. If whatsapp_status shows a gap in the period, silence may be missing data rather than nothing being said.

How to use it

  1. Download WhatsApp MCP and connect your WhatsApp with the QR code.

  2. Connect your AI tool: Claude Desktop and Claude Code with one click; the others with a ready-made text.

  3. Paste the text into the tool’s chat, replacing whatever is in square brackets. When a recipe has a time, it’s the AI tool that waits for it: use one that can schedule tasks.
